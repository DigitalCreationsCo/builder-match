Builder Match

Scout the best solo builders on X and offer them a shot at a 10X+ outcome at a speedrun company.

"Your work stands out. A speedrun founder is building something where you'd matter from day one. Want an intro?"

The best builders work alone, in public, and no company with momentum has found them yet. Their posts give them away: they ship fast, think clearly, show taste, and improve every week. Some build near speedrun, near other venture-backed companies, or inside early startups of their own, and left alone they risk a sub-scale outcome or a competitor to the network.

An agent reads public build-in-public posts, scores the standouts, and matches each with a speedrun founder, or someone considering founding, as a founding engineer, co-builder, or contributor. Intros are double opt-in. Speedrun companies get exceptional early builders, builders get a funded team and network behind a 10X+ shot, and those who would rather found are pointed to the fellowship.

  • Scout: score 1,000 recent build-in-public posts on shipping pace, clarity, taste, and improvement, and keep the top 3%.
  • Match: a speedrun partner pairs each builder with the best-fit founder and picks the offer: founding engineer, contributor, or fellowship.
  • Send and measure: DM from a real speedrun account, introduce only after both say yes, and track double-yes rate, calls, and joins.
